# Christian Before We Build Application

English · [[christian-application-overview-ru|Русский]] · [[christian-application-overview-uk|Українська]]

<!-- section:in-90-seconds -->
## In 90 seconds

Christian Before We Build is the first developed specialization of the universal framework. It offers a weak-AI-supported conversation map for Christian discernment before serious shared decisions. It interprets the foundation through Christ, Scripture, observable fruit, church community, and mature counsel while retaining consent and safety gates.

<!-- section:definition-and-scope -->
## Definition and scope

The application helps an individual or pair prepare questions, name disagreement, distinguish certainty from uncertainty, and identify wise next steps. It is not the whole research project, a dating oracle, pastoral office, spiritual diagnosis, or substitute for accountable community.

<!-- section:shared-example -->
## Shared example

For relocation, the application asks how biblical obligations to spouse, dependant, church, work, truth, and stewardship bear on the choice; how the pair understands vocation and time; how responsibilities will be carried; and whether information and consent are adequate.

<!-- section:observations -->
## What can be observed

The map can record stated beliefs, cited texts, advice received, decisions, conduct, repair, areas of agreement, unresolved questions, and whether each person can participate freely.

<!-- section:hypotheses -->
## What remains a hypothesis

The three typological lenses may help generate additional questions about direction, action, and information. Their interpretations remain optional research hypotheses subordinate to Scripture-grounded normative discernment and real evidence.

<!-- section:alternatives -->
## Alternative explanations

A disagreement attributed to type may instead concern doctrine, conscience, sin, wisdom, trauma, skill, resources, church culture, or an unsafe power imbalance. The application must route these possibilities correctly.

<!-- section:non-inferences -->
## What must not be inferred

No profile establishes faith, salvation, sanctification, calling, permission to marry, guilt, innocence, abuse, or God's hidden will. AI may summarize, organize, and propose questions; it may not simulate or predict a person.

<!-- section:conversation-questions -->
## Questions for conversation

- Which biblical commitments govern this decision?
- Which observations show fruit, responsibility, repentance, and repair over time?
- Where is mature pastoral or professional help required?
- Can both people disagree, pause, and seek help safely?

<!-- section:researcher-route -->
## For a researcher

Keep theological validity, pastoral usefulness, psychological measurement, and product usability as distinct evaluation questions. Record harms and false confidence, not only engagement.
