# Evidence Protocol for Testing Public-Figure Hypotheses

<!-- section:purpose -->
## Purpose

The protocol tests whether a provisional hypothesis about a type pattern of perception or experience organization survives criticism. It does not describe the whole personality, establish a natural predisposition, or turn public material into a spiritual, moral, pastoral, diagnostic, or staffing assessment. People lead the process; AI may only help organize verified records and formulate questions. See [[typology-reconceptualization-en]].

<!-- section:corpus-sufficiency-gates -->
## Corpus-sufficiency gates

| Corpus state | Permissible outcome |
|---|---|
| office, short biography, or short clips only | insufficient evidence |
| biography and one full item from one genre | tentative hypothesis with strong role-overfit risk |
| different full genres and years, including a spontaneous response or decision discussion | hypothesis may proceed to blind coding |
| voluntary interview or self-report, external observations, and independent reviewers | conditional support may be possible, but not a conclusion about a person's essence |

Diversity matters more than quantity. Several copies of the same representative biography do not form an independent corpus.

<!-- section:source-use-hierarchy -->
## Source-use hierarchy

| Source | Suitable use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| official biography | verifiable roles and dates | barely separates personal pattern from office |
| full sermon or lecture | structure of prepared explanation | topic and genre may imitate an aspect |
| interview | explanation of choice and self-description | self-presentation and interviewer influence |
| Q&A or discussion | response to questions, disagreement, and clarification | one episode is context-dependent |
| book or article | conceptual organization | editing and professional skill |
| crisis address | priorities under load | stress temporarily changes behaviour |
| social post or short clip | finding a full primary source | not a standalone basis for a code |
| external type or online test | a hypothesis to check when provenance is clear | does not replace evidence or psychometric evaluation |

<!-- section:evidence-row -->
## Evidence row

Each record stores:

- exact URL, date, genre, authorship, and editing;
- a short quotation or verifiable episode description;
- an observation without typological vocabulary;
- role, audience, crisis, and level of preparation;
- a typological interpretation and at least one non-typological alternative;
- evidence that would weaken the interpretation;
- a note: supports candidate, supports alternative, contradicts, or ambiguous.

A paraphrase, including an AI summary, never replaces the primary excerpt.

<!-- section:blind-coding -->
## Blind coding

Where possible, remove name, office, organization, and proposed code from the packet. Retain the excerpt, broad genre, and minimal context needed for meaning. Independent coders describe observations first and compare disagreements; name and role return only afterward.

Agreement is recorded as **no agreement**, **partial**, or **convergence**. It is not a quality coefficient. With no agreement, the result is “not distinguishable from current evidence.”

<!-- section:differentiating-questions -->
## Differentiating questions

The traditional descriptions below are system hypotheses, not established facts:

| Candidates | What to distinguish | Main confounder |
|---|---|---|
| LSE / LSI / ESI | work result; systemic norm; personal loyalty and boundary | administrative office |
| EII / LII | conscience and relationship; categories and consistency | teaching-pastor role |
| EIE / ESE | mobilization around a path; atmosphere and present care | conference theme |
| SEE / SLE / EIE | personal influence; organization of force and risk; meaning-based mobilization | chaplaincy or crisis |
| LVEF / LEVF | volitional or emotional-pedagogical support with leading Logic | teaching |
| VLFE / LVFE | direction first or model/principle first | leadership role |
| VFEL / FVEL | volitional frame or material-practical standard | craft and household competence |
| PFNE / FPNE | continuity frames the future or future mission rereads the past | institutional rhetoric |
| EPFN / PEFN | meaning organizes history or history organizes meaning | Christian vocabulary |
| FNPE / NFPE | future mission organizes the present or place discloses the future | church planting |

These questions guide data collection; the topic of a statement is not itself the answer.

<!-- section:qualitative-confidence-assessment -->
## Qualitative confidence assessment

The arbiter considers provenance breadth, genre diversity, spontaneity, stability over time, excerpt specificity, independent agreement, role overfit, and competing explanations. These factors are not added into a score.

Allowed states are **insufficient evidence**, **tentative hypothesis**, and **conditionally supported hypothesis**. Advancement requires new evidence, not a more persuasive retelling of the same material.

<!-- section:final-report -->
## Final report

The report contains a source list, excerpt table, blind observations, separate reviews of the three systems, differentiator results, strongest alternatives, qualitative confidence, and a revision condition. Moral, spiritual, and behavioural predictions are excluded.

Initial pilots prioritize dossiers for Serhii Sannikov, Mykola Romaniuk, and Ihor Bandura because sources and competing hypotheses have already been identified. See [[public-figure-typing-evidence-dossiers-batch-1-en]].
