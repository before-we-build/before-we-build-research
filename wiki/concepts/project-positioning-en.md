# Project Positioning

English · [[project-positioning-ru|Русский]] · [[project-positioning-uk|Українська]]

<!-- section:in-90-seconds -->
## In 90 seconds

Before We Build is the universal research core. Christian Before We Build is its first developed worldview and relationship application. Cognitive Matchmaker is a possible later dating track. An application may specify commitments and language, but it must not silently redefine the core.

<!-- section:definition-and-scope -->
## Definition and scope

The core owns the ontology, four-level map, evidence rules, context and safety constraints, and validation programme. An application owns its domain, normative sources, audience, practices, and decision boundaries.

<!-- section:shared-example -->
## Shared example

In relocation research, the universal core asks how to distinguish obligation, direction, action, and information. A Christian application may additionally ask how Scripture, vocation, church community, and mature counsel inform the obligation. Those answers are explicit specialization, not universal premises.

<!-- section:observations -->
## What can be observed

A repository page can be classified by the questions it answers, the sources it treats as normative, and the population for which its guidance is intended.

<!-- section:hypotheses -->
## What remains a hypothesis

The four-level architecture and typological mappings remain research proposals even when an application uses them confidently as conversation prompts.

<!-- section:alternatives -->
## Alternative explanations

Other worldview or professional applications may operationalize the foundation differently while preserving dignity, consent, responsibility, and safety. Different commitments need not be treated as interchangeable or equally true.

<!-- section:non-inferences -->
## What must not be inferred

Universal does not mean value-free. Christian does not mean that the entire repository is a church or dating product. Application guidance does not become empirical evidence merely by being useful.

<!-- section:conversation-questions -->
## Questions for conversation

- Is this claim universal, worldview-specific, or domain-specific?
- Which authority or evidence supports it?
- Would the statement remain meaningful for another kind of pair?
- Is the reader being asked to investigate, believe, or act?
