# Value-Moral Compatibility

Languages: **English** | [[value-moral-compatibility-ru|Русский]] | [[value-moral-compatibility-uk|Українська]]

## Status

Value-moral compatibility is a **universal Before We Build project
construct** for studying any two people. It is not a complete moral theory,
religious doctrine, personality typology, psychometric moral-worth scale, or
automated verdict.

## Definition

Value-moral compatibility asks:

> Are the values, moral obligations, and non-negotiable boundaries of two
> people compatible enough for this particular relationship or shared task?

The domain must be named. Two people may be compatible as neighbors or
colleagues but incompatible as spouses, co-founders, caregivers, leaders, or
partners with shared finances.

## Main Components

| Component | Pair-level question |
|---|---|
| Worldview | What sources of meaning and moral authority does each person recognize? |
| Human dignity | How is the worth, agency, and vulnerability of each person treated? |
| Truth and honesty | What disclosure, accuracy, confidentiality, and candor does the relationship require? |
| Commitments | Which promises, duties, roles, and forms of fidelity are binding? |
| Consent and autonomy | Can each person freely agree, refuse, renegotiate, or leave within legitimate constraints? |
| Responsibility | Who is accountable for consequences, dependents, resources, and repair? |
| Reciprocity and fairness | What asymmetry is legitimate, and what becomes exploitation? |
| Care and repair | What happens after harm, error, disappointment, or broken trust? |
| Non-negotiable boundaries | Which differences cannot be solved by compromise in this domain? |
| Safety | Are coercion, abuse, violence, exploitation, or severe power misuse present? |

## Compatibility Is Not Identical Values

The level should distinguish:

1. **Shared commitments** — both endorse and practice a relevant value.
2. **Compatible difference** — values differ but can coexist without
   deception, coercion, or structural contradiction.
3. **Negotiable implementation** — the underlying commitment is compatible,
   but timing, roles, or methods require agreement.
4. **Non-negotiable conflict** — the relationship requires mutually
   contradictory obligations or violations of essential boundaries.
5. **Safety failure** — coercion, abuse, violence, exploitation, or inability
   to give meaningful consent overrides ordinary compatibility scoring.

## Evidence Model

Use at least three evidence classes:

- **stated commitments** — what each person says they value or owe;
- **choice under conflict** — what takes priority when values compete;
- **observed conduct and repair** — repeated behavior, accountability, and
  response after failure.

Self-report alone is insufficient. Behavior alone also needs context: power,
fear, law, dependency, health, culture, role expectations, and available
choices may affect what is observed.

## Application Contract

Every implementation of this level should declare:

```yaml
domain: relationship | friendship | family | team | business | care | other
population_and_culture: []
worldview_sources: []
legal_or_professional_constraints: []
relevant_commitments: []
non_negotiable_boundaries: []
power_differences: []
safety_gates: [consent, coercion, abuse, violence, exploitation]
evidence_window: []
```

Without this contract, “value compatibility” becomes vague and culturally
untraceable.

## Relationship to Worldview Applications

The universal construct does not prescribe one worldview answer set.

- [[spiritual-foundation-levels-ru]] is a Christian specialization that
  interprets the level through Christ, Scripture, fruit, repentance, church
  community, and mature counsel.
- Other applications must identify their own sources accurately.
- Universal scope does not assert that all worldviews are equally true or
  compatible.

## Research and Measurement Boundary

Future research may compare:

- direct value agreement;
- observed moral reliability;
- perceived fairness and reciprocity;
- commitment clarity;
- repair after violations;
- domain-specific outcomes;
- incremental prediction beyond attachment, conflict style, personality
  traits, life stage, and social context.

No combined score should hide a safety failure or become a score of human
worth.

## Non-Claims

This construct does not establish:

- which worldview is ultimately true;
- whether a person is good, saved, holy, civilized, or worthy;
- whether every value difference requires separation;
- whether stated agreement guarantees reliable conduct;
- whether high compatibility guarantees a successful relationship;
- whether typology can infer values or moral character.
