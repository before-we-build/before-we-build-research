# Main Idea

Before We Build is a universal compatibility research framework for studying
how any two people may build, decide, coordinate, and sustain a relationship
or shared task. It interprets Socionics, Psychosophy, and Temporistics as
heuristic models of latent psychological processes rather than final
explanations of a person. A type is treated as an observable trace and working
hypothesis about how hidden processes may be organized.

The first developed application is a Christian relationship-discernment
conversation map, but that application does not define or exhaust the
universal research core. See [[project-main-goal]] and
[[research-layer-vs-practical-guidance]].

## 1. Central Mapping

| Typology | Compatibility level | Latent process class | Frame type | Main question |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Socionics** | Tactical | Information modeling | Information frame | How does a person perceive, filter, structure, and exchange information? |
| **Psychosophy** | Operational | Synthesis and analysis in action | Action frame | How does a person organize decisions, effort, pressure, emotion, logic, will, and practical embodiment? |
| **Temporistics** | Strategic | Induction and deduction in temporal/existential experience | Temporal frame | How does a person organize Past, Present, Future, and Eternity into meaning, continuity, direction, and life trajectory? |

Required formula:

- **Socionics → latent processes of information modeling**
- **Psychosophy → latent processes of synthesis and analysis in action**
- **Temporistics → latent processes of induction and deduction in temporal/existential experience**

## 2. Why Typologies Matter Here

The value of typologies in Before We Build is not primarily that they classify people into labels. Their value is that type structures may serve as compact hypotheses about deeper latent processes.

In this interpretation:

```text
type label ≠ final explanation
type label = structured hypothesis about latent process organization
```

The practical question is not only “What type is this person?” but also:

- What hidden process does this pattern suggest?
- Which observable behaviors support or contradict the hypothesis?
- What predictions does the hypothesis generate?
- How can those predictions be tested or corrected?

## 3. Observable Traces vs Latent Processes

Before We Build distinguishes behavior from mechanism.

Observable traces include speech patterns, choices, conflict behavior, planning habits, stress responses, and explanations people give for their actions. Latent processes are proposed hidden mechanisms reconstructed from those traces.

The intended chain is:

```text
observable traces
→ typological pattern
→ latent process hypothesis
→ compatibility or role-fit hypothesis
→ validation against outcomes
```

See [[latent-process]] for the detailed framework.

## 4. The Four Compatibility Levels

Before We Build uses a four-level compatibility architecture: one
value-moral foundation and three typological research levels.

1. **Value-moral compatibility** asks which values, moral obligations, and non-negotiable boundaries govern what two people build. Its evidence includes stated commitments, observed conduct, consent and safety, and relevant worldview, community, and institutional context. It is not a typology or automated moral verdict.
2. **Strategic compatibility** asks whether two people can share a compatible long-range temporal and existential direction. The primary typology is **Temporistics**.
3. **Operational compatibility** asks whether two people can organize decisions, effort, pressure, priorities, and embodiment together. The primary typology is **Psychosophy**.
4. **Tactical compatibility** asks whether two people can perceive, exchange, repair, and coordinate information in concrete situations. The primary typology is **Socionics**.

The value-moral foundation cannot be inferred from a type, and the three
typological levels should not be collapsed into each other. Similar words
across systems do not mean identical constructs. For example, Psychosophy
Emotion is not Socionics Fe or Fi, Psychosophy Will is not Socionics Se, and
Temporistics Future is not Socionics Ni. See
[[four-level-compatibility-architecture]],
[[value-moral-compatibility]], and [[compatibility-level-boundaries]].

## 5. Positional Modes

Across position-based systems, Before We Build uses the concept of a positional mode: a relatively stable way of working with a frame.

| Position | Heuristic interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Autonomous generation of the frame |
| 2 | Cooperative modification of the frame |
| 3 | Reconstruction of the frame under deficit, contradiction, or uncertainty |
| 4 | Acceptance of an externally given frame |

This is a project interpretation and should not be confused with canonical doctrine in every source tradition.

## 6. What Before We Build Is Not Claiming

Before We Build is a research framework, not a deterministic personality doctrine. It does not claim that:

- typological systems are scientifically proven as complete models of personality;
- a type fully explains a person;
- compatibility is fate;
- one typological system can be directly translated into another;
- social context, development, culture, health, or life circumstances can be ignored.

The framework should use careful language: hypothesis, interpretation, heuristic model, proposed mapping, latent process model, and testable direction.

## 7. Framework vs Application

**Before We Build** is the universal compatibility research architecture.

The Christian conversation map is the first developed worldview/domain
application. The earlier **Cognitive Matchmaker** idea is a future dating
research track, not the universal core or near MVP.

See [[project-main-goal]] and [[project-positioning]] for the full distinction.
