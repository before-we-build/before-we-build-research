# Working Hypotheses About Public Figures Relevant to Ukraine's Baptist Audience

<!-- section:status-and-ethical-boundary -->
## Status and ethical boundary

This is an internal historical snapshot of research hypotheses based on limited public material. It does not establish a “person's type,” provide a psychometric assessment, or evaluate faith, morality, pastoral fitness, or authority. Public office strongly confounds role and behaviour, so every code below remains a low-confidence candidate.

The page tests a method: can observation be separated from interpretation, alternatives retained, and a code withheld when evidence is insufficient? It is not intended for public ranking or trust decisions.

<!-- section:source-base -->
## Source base

The official UECB leadership page supplies offices, education, and ministry histories. AUCCRO adds information on Valerii Antoniuk; a spiritual-fatherhood article on Ihor Bandura; and encyclopaedic pages on Serhii Sannikov and Hennadii Mokhnenko. These sources support biographical facts, not typological codes.

<!-- section:what-supports-and-weakens-the-table -->
## What supports and weakens the table

Only recurring thematic traces provide support: organization, teaching, mission, historical continuity, work with a future generation, and church planting. Staged genres, website editing, common demands of church office, a small sample of spontaneous speech, and no self-report weaken every inference.

A topic must not substitute for a function: teaching does not establish Logic, a leadership office Volition, expressive preaching Emotion, or a mission project Future. Each code would require episodes of choice, error, conflict, role change, and decision revision.

<!-- section:what-to-collect-next -->
## What to collect next

- full interviews and sermons across genres and years;
- exact quotations with date, link, and context;
- episodes in which a person explains a choice, acknowledges error, or responds to uncertainty;
- independent blind coding of observations before revealing name and office;
- evidence that differentiates primary and alternative codes;
- explicit alternatives: role, theological school, culture, age, crisis, and editing.

Without such a corpus, “insufficient evidence” is the correct result.

<!-- section:non-inferences -->
## Non-inferences

The codes do not establish spirituality, character, safety, vocation, dignity, suitability for marriage, or relationship outcome. The hypotheses must not be used for church appointment, public reputational assessment, or attribution of hidden motives.
