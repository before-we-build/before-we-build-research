# Historical First Review of Hypotheses About Four Public Figures — 2026-06-21

<!-- section:status -->
## Status

This is a historical record of the first review, not a current typological assessment. The later protocol found the corpus insufficient for confident codes and replaced automated “agent” arbitration with human-led review. The table is retained to show which candidates and errors needed further differentiation.

<!-- section:ontology-supersession -->
## Ontology supersession notice

These Socionics candidates predate the BWB distinction between aspect operation and Model A position mode. They have not been recalculated under [[socionics-reality-modeling-en]] and must not be treated as evidence for the new ontology.

In the current reading these are **tentative candidates**, and evidence for an exact code is insufficient.

## What was directly supported

| Figure | Biographical and textual traces | What they do not support |
|---|---|---|
| Serhii Sannikov | theology, church history, books, education, editing, and accreditation | a particular information function, position, or temporal mode |
| Mykola Romaniuk | philology, Bible education, teaching pastorate, church history, and minister development | whether an ethical or structural-logical process leads |
| Valerii Antoniuk | pastoral, regional, executive, and union leadership and representation | personal pattern outside office demands |
| Ihor Bandura | music, evangelism, church planting, mission, orphans, and spiritual fatherhood | that the generation theme is stable outside its genre |

<!-- section:errors-and-alternatives -->
## Errors and alternatives

- An academic occupation can resemble leading Logic while actually showing skill and subject matter.
- A teaching pastorate simultaneously requires relationship, conceptual clarity, and emotional support.
- Leadership office requires organization and responsibility regardless of type.
- Mission and conference genres foreground future, inspiration, and direction.
- Christian language about eternity does not establish a Temporistics position.

<!-- section:what-changed-after-the-run -->
## What changed after the run

The current process requires exact quotations, different genres and years, de-identified coding, independent human reviewers, non-typological alternatives, and an explicit revision condition. AI may only summarize and organize verified material; it does not choose a type or predict a person.

<!-- section:non-inferences -->
## Non-inferences

Historical codes must not support judgments about faith, morality, safety, pastoral fitness, authority, staffing, or relationship future. See [[public-figure-typing-evidence-protocol-en]] and [[public-figure-typing-evidence-dossiers-batch-1-en]].
