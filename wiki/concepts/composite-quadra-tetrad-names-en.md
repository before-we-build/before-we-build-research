# Composite Quadra and Tetrad Nomenclature

This page defines a technical way to name a combination of one Socionics quadra, one Psychosophy grouping, and one Temporistics tetrad. It is navigation nomenclature, not a psychological classification of people, compatibility group, or behavioural prediction.

<!-- section:1-combinatorial-space -->
## 1. Combinatorial space

The current registry contains four Socionics quadras, six Psychosophy groups, and six Temporistics tetrads. Their Cartesian product contains `4 × 6 × 6 = 144` composite codes. This number describes labels, not natural population classes.

<!-- section:2-status-of-names -->
## 2. Status of names

| Layer | Nomenclature | BWB status |
|---|---|---|
| Socionics | Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta | quadra names from the Socionics tradition |
| Psychosophy | six P1–P6 groups | later/community grouping; not presented as Afanasyev's canon |
| Temporistics | six T1–T6 tetrads | names attributed to `raw/temporistics/types.md` |

Working translations support navigation. They do not turn a source metaphor into an observed property of a person.

<!-- section:4-composite-name-rule -->
## 4. Composite-name rule

Machine code: `S{1–4}-P{1–6}-T{1–6}`.

A human-readable record lists the three attributed names without adding a psychological characteristic. For example:

```text
S4-P2-T4 — Delta / Household organization / Проводники
```

The previous enumeration of 144 long descriptions is replaced by this reproducible rule: it repeated combinations mechanically and could be misread as ready-made portraits.

<!-- section:5-permitted-use -->
## 5. Permitted use

- group research records;
- check coverage of code space;
- link to the three source groups;
- generate hypotheses when each proposed mechanism is stated and tested separately.

Do not use a composite name as a kind of person, moral characteristic, compatibility claim, role, diagnosis, explanation of conduct, or automated decision.
