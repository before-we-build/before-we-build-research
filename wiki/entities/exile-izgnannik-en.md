English · [[exile-izgnannik-ru|Русский]] · [[exile-izgnannik-uk|Українська]]

<!-- section:definition -->
**3N** places **Present** in position 3 of Temporistics. This is a strategic temporal-existential hypothesis, not a diagnosis, spiritual status, personality essence, or prediction.

<!-- section:meaning -->
## Compact meaning

**Exile** is a traditional working alias for **3N / Present**. The position is modeled as a sensitive error-monitoring and revision zone. The alias is a mnemonic, not an observable kind of person.

## How to read it

Compare the hypothesis with repeated decisions and stories, counterexamples, and alternative codes. Separate a stable pattern from role, crisis, culture, skill, health, and circumstance.
