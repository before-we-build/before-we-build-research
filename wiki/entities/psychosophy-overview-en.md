# Psychosophy: Operational Reference Overview

<!-- section:overview-and-scope -->
## Overview and Scope

<!-- claim:psychosophy-operational-model -->
Psychosophy is a typological theory associated with Alexander Afanasyev and *Syntax of Love*. Before We Build uses it as a **heuristic hypothesis about the operational organization of joint action**: how people negotiate agency, expression, reasons, material resources, effort, and pressure. It is not a complete theory of personality and does not determine relationship outcomes.

<!-- section:core-constructs -->
## Core Constructs

The system orders four aspects. The BWB wording below is an operational interpretation, not an empirically established mechanism.

| Aspect | Reference question | Operational hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Will / Volition (V) | Who decides, commits, and sets boundaries? | organization of agency, goals, and participation |
| Emotion (E) | How is expression or atmosphere formed and received? | organization of emotional expression in joint action |
| Logic (L) | How are reasons formed, tested, and exchanged? | organization of reasoning and justification |
| Physics (F) | How are material and bodily needs handled? | organization of practical resources, comfort, and maintenance |

<!-- section:position-structure -->
## Position Structure

| Position | Common school label | Cautious process hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | confident / result-oriented | tends to present an already assembled frame |
| 2nd | flexible / process-oriented | tends to develop a frame through dialogue |
| 3rd | vulnerable / process-oriented | may monitor the domain closely and experience pressure there |
| 4th | low-priority / result-oriented | may accept an external frame or invest less attention |

These descriptions must be tested in episodes. “Confident,” “vulnerable,” and “low-priority” describe proposed handling of a domain, not competence, dignity, or moral character.

<!-- section:type-reference -->
## Type Reference

<!-- claim:psychosophy-reference-codes -->
The standard reference catalog contains the 24 permutations of L, E, V, and F. A code such as `LEVF` means 1L, 2E, 3V, and 4F; it is a compact model label, not a kind of human being. Afanasyev's theoretical “25th type” (`2-2-2-2`) is retained as a source-specific historical proposal, not as an additional observed category.

<!-- section:observations-and-alternatives -->
## Observations and Alternative Explanations

Directly observable material includes who initiates a decision, asks for discussion, changes a plan, handles practical work, or reacts to pressure in a specific episode. A Psychosophy position is an interpretation of repeated indicators. Skill, role, power, culture, attachment, health, stress, resources, and the concrete task are rival explanations.

<!-- section:non-inferences -->
## What Must Not Be Inferred

- A position does not measure intelligence, morality, emotional depth, willpower, health, consent, or human worth.
- A type does not fix character, vocation, safety, spiritual maturity, or destiny.
- A type or position does not create a pair score, ranking, recommendation, or verdict.
- Public-person typings and school attributions are hypotheses or historical claims unless independently validated.

<!-- section:bwb-use -->
## Use in Before We Build

Psychosophy supplies operational questions: How will we decide? Who needs dialogue before commitment? How do we share practical work? When does help become pressure? Answers come from observed interaction, explicit consent, and context; the type code only organizes hypotheses.
