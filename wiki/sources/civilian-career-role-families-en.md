# Civilian Career Role Families

This page describes the shared role-family catalog used by `civilian-career-advisor`. It translates broad role labels into observable work requirements for evidence-first career exploration.

The catalog is not an exhaustive labor-market database or an automatic selection tool. Skills, work samples, results, credentials, preferences, constraints, location, compensation needs, and current vacancies are the primary evidence. If a typological hypothesis is retained for research, it may add a question only after that direct evidence is considered; it must not rank or exclude careers.

<!-- section:use-in-career-exploration -->
## Use In Career Exploration

1. Gather verified background: work samples, tools, portfolio, education, outcomes, preferences, and constraints.
2. Convert candidate roles into observable work requirements.
3. Compare the evidence and identify gaps that a trial task, accommodation, or training could test.
4. If a typological hypothesis is retained for research, use it only to generate an additional question after the direct evidence has been considered.

<!-- section:evidence-quality -->
## Evidence Quality

Confidence concerns evidence for a specific role requirement, not agreement with a type profile. Work samples, observed performance, qualifications, accessibility needs, informed choice, location, compensation, and current market conditions are relevant. A typology result does not raise the confidence level and must not drive ranking, hiring, or exclusion.

## Source Assessment

<!-- section:source-claims -->
### What the Source Claims

The catalog proposes grouping civilian roles by observable work-feature tags so that career conversations can compare actual demands rather than job titles alone.

<!-- section:source-evidence -->
### Data or Evidence Provided

Its evidence is the maintained role catalog and practical occupational descriptions; it includes no longitudinal hiring or job-performance dataset.

<!-- section:source-limitations -->
### Limitations

Coverage is incomplete and changes by labor market, location, employer, seniority, and accommodation. The tags are not a validated selection instrument.

<!-- section:bwb-accepts -->
### What BWB Accepts

BWB accepts feature-based role exploration, portfolio evidence, qualifications, constraints, and direct work trials as useful inputs.

<!-- section:bwb-contested -->
### What Remains Contested or Open

The incremental value of any typological hypothesis for role fit remains open and must be tested beyond skills and context.

<!-- section:bwb-rejected-or-historical -->
### Rejected or Historical-Only

BWB rejects automated career assignment, exclusion by type, and allowing a profile to override observed performance or informed choice.
