# Mosaic: potential competitor and naming conflict

<!-- section:status -->
## Status

`Mosaic` is tracked as a **potential competitor and material naming conflict**. It refers to several separate products in relationship analysis, couple support, and psychometric assessment. This is a snapshot from **2026-07-27**; marketing claims, user counts, accuracy, and effectiveness have not been independently confirmed.

<!-- section:1-mosaicchats-closest-competitor -->
## 1. MosaicChats — closest competitor

- **URL:** https://www.mosaicchats.com/
- **Privacy:** https://www.mosaicchats.com/privacy-policy
- **Observed positioning:** “Relationship Intelligence” based on AI analysis of exported conversations.
- **Claimed outputs:** sentiment and interaction patterns,
  <!-- claim-audit: allow reason=competitor-source-attribution -->
  compatibility score, MBTI analysis, relationship report, and AI-coach recommendations.
- **Announced direction:** MosaicMatch and Amavie, matching inferred from communication style and personality.

<!-- section:why-it-overlaps -->
### Why it overlaps

Both domains turn traces of interaction into a structured relationship report and discuss communication patterns, personal differences, and matching. MosaicChats begins with existing messages; BWB begins with consensual answers, guided questions, observations, and an explicit separation between evidence and hypothesis.

<!-- section:material-differences -->
### Material differences

| Dimension | MosaicChats | Before We Build |
|---|---|---|
| Input | exported private conversations | voluntary answers, questions, and observations |
| Object | communication visible in messages | moral-value foundation, temporal direction, joint action, information exchange |
<!-- claim-audit: allow reason=competitor-source-attribution -->
| Public output | compatibility score, MBTI, report, AI coach | conversation map, differences, open questions, next wise step |
| Epistemic stance | marketing uses “true compatibility” language | provisional hypotheses without verdict or fate prediction |
| Market | dating and relationship advice | any pair preparing for a serious shared decision; first application is Christian |

<!-- section:2-mosaic-cultural-compass-for-couples -->
## 2. Mosaic — Cultural Compass for Couples

- **URL:** https://www.mosaicglobalapp.com/
- **Observed positioning:** intercultural relationship support through Cultural DNA Assessment, Loyalty Map, and conversations about family, money, parenting, culture, and communication.

This is closer to BWB's “shared language for differences” format but has a narrower intercultural specialization.

<!-- section:3-mosaic-personality-tasks -->
## 3. Mosaic Personality Tasks

- **URL:** https://www.mosaictasks.com/
- **Organization:** Mosaic Assessments Ltd.
- **Observed positioning:** professional multimethod psychometrics combining self-report with behavioral tasks, based on Big Five and work competencies.

It is not a direct relationship competitor, but it is a relevant assessment brand, a methodological comparison point, and another reason not to adopt `Mosaic` as BWB's name.

<!-- section:privacy-and-consent-risk -->
## Privacy and Consent Risk

MosaicChats' policy acknowledges third-party messages and inferred behavioral/personality information. BWB should require consent of affected people, data minimization, no covert profiling, clear upload rules, separation of observed text from inference, and deletion of source and derived data.

<!-- section:what-to-monitor -->
## What to Monitor

1. Movement toward MosaicMatch and Amavie.
2. Public methodology and independent validation.
3. Privacy/consent changes for non-user messages.
4. Monetization and shareable reports.
<!-- claim-audit: allow reason=competitor-source-attribution -->
5. MBTI, compatibility scores, and high-confidence marketing language.
6. Expansion of Cultural Compass into faith, values, or premarital preparation.
7. Trademark and search occupancy of `Mosaic`.

<!-- section:before-we-build-decision -->
## Before We Build Decision

Treat MosaicChats as the closest potential AI relationship-intelligence competitor, Cultural Compass as adjacent, and Personality Tasks as a psychometric reference and naming conflict. Do not adopt `Mosaic` without legal review.

<!-- claim-audit: allow reason=competitor-source-attribution -->
Do not copy public compatibility scores, MBTI-from-chat claims, or conversation uploads without clear consent from every participant.

## Source Assessment

<!-- section:source-claims -->
### What the Source Claims

Mosaic's public materials claim that uploaded conversations can support automated relationship analysis, personality-style outputs, reports, and coaching.

<!-- section:source-evidence -->
### Data or Evidence Provided

The summary uses public product and marketing materials; it does not include independent outcome validation, calibration data, or access to the provider's full methods.

<!-- section:source-limitations -->
### Limitations

Marketing descriptions cannot establish accuracy, causal benefit, privacy practice in every deployment, or safety for high-risk relationship contexts.

<!-- section:bwb-accepts -->
### What BWB Accepts

BWB accepts the competitor review as a product-boundary benchmark, especially for consent, provenance, uncertainty, and non-diagnostic conversation support.

<!-- section:bwb-contested -->
### What Remains Contested or Open

The accuracy, incremental benefit, privacy implementation, and harm profile of Mosaic's outputs remain open without independent evaluation.

<!-- section:bwb-rejected-or-historical -->
### Rejected or Historical-Only

BWB rejects copying automated pair rankings, inferring a person's type from private conversation, or issuing relationship verdicts.
