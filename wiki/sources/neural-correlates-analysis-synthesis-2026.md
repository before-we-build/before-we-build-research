# Neural Correlates of Analysis and Integration — Research Note (2026)

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This derived source note collects neuroimaging reviews and meta-analyses used
by [[neural-correlates-analysis-synthesis-ru]]. It does **not** establish a
neural basis for Psychosophy, Socionics, Temporistics, or any position within
them. The studies concern laboratory tasks, not typological types.

In this note, *analysis* is a project shorthand for distinguishing and
maintaining relevant elements, rules, constraints, or relations. *Synthesis*
is a project shorthand for integrating them into a higher-order relation,
valuation, interpretation, or action plan. These are not standard names for
unitary brain modules.

## Evidence ledger

| Domain / operation | Evidence | Narrow conclusion supported | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deductive reasoning | [Quantitative meta-analysis of 28 studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21568632/) | Different deductive arguments consistently recruit a left fronto-parietal system and left basal ganglia, with task-specific subsystems. | Does not locate a general human faculty of “analysis.” |
| Inductive rule acquisition | [fMRI study of rule acquisition and following](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21613489/) | Mid-DLPFC was active during rule search and until successful acquisition; frontopolar cortex was active while an unfamiliar rule was acquired and followed. | A single task study is not a general neural map of induction. |
| Abduction / inference to the best explanation | [Methodological review in cognitive neuroscience](https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/20134/1/Final%20Manuscript.pdf) | In neuroimaging, reasoning backwards from activation to a cognitive process is itself an abductive, methodologically contested inference. | We did not identify a mature, convergent neuroimaging map for human abduction comparable to the deduction meta-analyses; do not invent one. |
| Mental time travel: autobiographical past and episodic future | [Updated ALE meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40312608/) | Both past remembering and future simulation involve midline structures, bilateral hippocampus/parahippocampus, angular gyrus, anterior middle temporal gyrus, and left superior frontal gyrus; future thinking also showed a right aMTG difference. | This concerns episodic remembering/imagination, not a typological temporal frame or duration perception. |
| Prospection and long-horizon choice | [Meta-analysis of episodic future thinking interventions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34843367/) | Vivid, positive, delayed-choice-relevant future episodes can shift intertemporal choice toward larger delayed outcomes. | It does not measure an enduring Future-oriented type or establish a moral hierarchy of long-term planning. |
| Working-memory maintenance and manipulation | [Coordinate meta-analysis of 189 fMRI experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3288533/) | A broad bilateral fronto-parietal network recurs across working-memory tasks. | The network supports multiple operations, not reasoning alone. |
| Relational integration / analogy | [Review and synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32762521/) | Posterior parietal cortex represents first-order relations; rostrolateral PFC is implicated in integrating relations; DLPFC supports working-memory maintenance and VLPFC interference control. | “Synthesis” in this project is broader than relational integration. |
| Cognitive control across tasks | [Frontoparietal-network review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6136121/) | The frontoparietal network flexibly coordinates information processing with other networks under changing task demands. | Network labels and regional borders vary by method and individual. |
| Value-based decision | [Meta-analysis of 206 fMRI studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23507394/) | vmPFC and anterior ventral striatum commonly track subjective value; insula, dmPFC, striatum, and thalamus also participate in salience/arousal-related patterns. | A value signal is not a complete account of agency, morality, or a decision. |
| Effortful choice | [Meta-analysis of 45 studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41688801/) | mPFC/dACC, putamen, and anterior insula are recruited when reward and cognitive demand are processed; caudal mPFC/dACC may integrate demand and reward. | This 2026 result is informative but does not prove a single “effort calculator.” |
| Multi-step planning / logistics-like puzzles | [Tower of London meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27627877/) | Planning and planning complexity recruit bilateral mid-DLPFC, frontal eye fields, supplementary motor area, precuneus, caudate, anterior insula, inferior parietal cortex, and left rostrolateral PFC. | A laboratory planning puzzle is only a partial analogue of real household, financial, or material logistics. |
| Spatial route planning | [Review of cognitive maps](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29073650/) | Hippocampus and entorhinal cortex support map-like spatial codes; parahippocampal and retrosplenial cortices anchor maps to landmarks; frontal mechanisms support route plans. | Navigating a route is not identical to organizing possessions, money, food, health, or home maintenance. |
| Interoception / bodily state in decisions | [ALE and connectivity meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35872175/) | Right dorsal anterior insula overlaps across interoception, decision-making, and emotion regulation; dACC is shared by decision-making and emotion regulation; the coactivation pattern includes bilateral insula, dACC, and thalamus. | This is not an anatomical definition of a project “Physics” aspect or proof of greater bodily awareness. |
| Spoken interaction / tactical coordination | [Systematic review of hyperscanning in spoken communication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33022298/) | During spoken interaction, studies report interpersonal neural alignment, predominantly in frontal and temporo-parietal areas, across turn-taking, knowledge sharing, cooperation, and discussion paradigms. | Alignment is not a direct measure of truth, consent, mutual understanding, or relationship quality. |
| Dyadic autonomic regulation | [HRV meta-analysis in social interaction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25559773/) | Negative dyadic interactions reduce HRV on average; the literature does not show that social interaction generally raises HRV from baseline. | HRV is context-, person-, and measurement-dependent; it is not a compatibility score. |
| Stress as a cross-level constraint | [Systematic review and meta-analysis of allostatic load and cognition](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32892066/) | Higher allostatic load is associated cross-sectionally with poorer global cognition and executive function; effects are small. | This evidence is not dyadic and does not establish causality or a type-specific vulnerability. |
| Interpersonal synchrony: interpretation limit | [Systematic review of interpersonal autonomic physiology](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26921410/) | Physiological activity can become interdependent across people in many relationship contexts, but the field is fragmented and methods vary. | Synchrony may reflect shared task demands, shared stress, co-regulation, or co-dysregulation; it is not inherently good. |
| Cognitive reappraisal of emotion | [Amygdala–prefrontal connectivity meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33516732/) | Reappraisal reliably modulates amygdala connectivity with VLPFC; down-regulation also shows connectivity with DLPFC and dmPFC. | This is one emotion-regulation strategy, not emotion as a whole. |
| Reappraisal versus acceptance | [ALE meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37546477/) | Both strategies showed increased left inferior frontal gyrus and insula, but their patterns differ. | Do not assume every healthy regulation strategy is top-down control. |
| Emotion representation | [Review of multivariate evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27133227/) | Affective dimensions and emotion categories are represented in distributed cortical and subcortical patterns. | It rules against simple one-emotion/one-region maps; it does not make any psychological taxonomy biologically validated. |

## Measurement warning

Task fMRI usually measures the BOLD signal: a delayed, indirect indicator of
local blood oxygenation related to neural activity. It is not a direct image of
a thought, a trait, a latent process, or ATP use. PET, EEG/MEG, lesions,
behavior, and connectivity analyses answer different parts of the question.

## Safe project use

- Use these results to design domain-specific tasks and candidate outcome
  measures.
- Treat the findings as mechanism-adjacent context, not type markers.
- Pre-register any claim that a project scale predicts task performance or
  neural measures; compare it with non-typological explanations such as
  ability, training, stress, sleep, current mood, and task familiarity.
