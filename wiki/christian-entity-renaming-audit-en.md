# Christian Entity Renaming Audit

<!-- section:purpose -->
## Purpose

This audit decides which Before We Build research terms may appear, need translation, or should stay in the research route when writing for a Christian public audience, especially Scripture-first readers.

It complements [[glossary-audience-translation-en]]:

- the terminology map defines audience-facing translations;
- this audit applies those translations to concrete entity families and page routes.

The Christian application is the first developed specialization of a universal research core. It must state its Christian commitments clearly without making Christian wording the definition of the universal core.

<!-- section:decision-categories -->
## Decision Categories

- **KEEP** — suitable and useful in ordinary Christian-facing language.
- **RENAME** — preserve the idea but use human, Scripture-near wording.
- **DEMOTE** — introduce only in a deeper explanation with caveats.
- **RESEARCH ONLY** — do not use as the first doorway.
- **PASTOR CHECK** — require human theological or pastoral review because doctrine, spiritual authority, church care, or Scripture interpretation is involved.

<!-- section:first-doorway-decisions -->
## First-Doorway Decisions

| Research term | Decision | Christian-facing wording | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| compatibility | **RENAME** | questions before a serious step; map for conversation | avoids a verdict or universal pair property |
| result | **RENAME** | themes to discuss; next questions | directs attention to conversation rather than classification |
| profile | **RENAME** | notes for conversation; map of differences | a person is more than a profile |
| test | **RENAME / DEMOTE** | guided questions; questions for discernment | does not imply spiritual or clinical judgment |
| type | **DEMOTE** | repeated pattern to examine | a type is a model hypothesis, not a kind of person |
| typology | **DEMOTE / RESEARCH ONLY** | limited map of differences | should not be the Christian first doorway |
| latent process | **RENAME / DEMOTE** | repeated pattern that may operate beneath the surface | does not claim access to the heart or a proven cause |
| model | **RENAME / DEMOTE** | limited working map | prevents a human framework from sounding authoritative |
| hypothesis | **DEMOTE** | working thought to test | keeps uncertainty visible |
| validation / measurement | **RESEARCH ONLY** | how we check whether the map is reliable and useful | methodological detail belongs in the research route |
| prediction | **RENAME / DEMOTE** | possible pattern to watch and test | avoids claiming knowledge of another person's future |
| diagnosis / ideal match / automated verdict | **RESEARCH EXCLUSION** | do not use | outside the project's authority and safety boundary |

There is no current numerical BWB compatibility score. Christian-facing pages must not suggest one through percentages, ranks, weights, or labels such as high/medium/low pair compatibility.

<!-- section:christian-foundations-to-keep-central -->
## Christian Foundations to Keep Central

| Entity | Decision | Christian-facing wording | Possible Scripture anchors | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| foundation | **KEEP / STRENGTHEN** | foundation before God; what we build on | Matt 7:24–25 | not a typological level |
| Scripture | **KEEP / STRENGTHEN** | examine claims by Scripture | 2 Tim 3:16–17; Acts 17:11 | cite rather than invoke vaguely |
| wisdom | **KEEP** | ask for and practice wisdom | Jas 1:5; Prov 11:14 | wisdom is not a test output |
| mature counsel | **KEEP** | wise counsel from trustworthy people and church community | Prov 11:14; 15:22 | counsel must not become coercion |
| prayer | **KEEP** | prayer before serious decisions | Jas 1:5 | not a product step or automatic confirmation |
| character and fruit | **KEEP / STRENGTHEN** | observable character, fruit, truthfulness, and responsibility | Matt 7:16–20; Gal 5:22–23 | type does not determine fruit |
| dignity | **KEEP / STRENGTHEN** | every person exceeds every map | Gen 1:26–27; 1 Sam 16:7 | no ranking of worth |
| faithfulness and repair | **KEEP** | fidelity, forgiveness, restitution, rebuilding trust | Col 3:12–14; Eph 4:31–32 | forgiveness does not erase safety boundaries |
| one body, many members | **KEEP / STRENGTHEN** | different gifts and mutual need | 1 Cor 12:13–22; Rom 12:4–5 | difference does not assign spiritual rank |

<!-- section:four-level-public-translation -->
## Four-Level Public Translation

| BWB architecture | Christian-facing question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Value-moral foundation | What are we building before God, and what do commitments, conduct, consent, safety, responsibility, reciprocity, and repair show? | foundation is normative, not Temporistics or a moral-worth test |
| Strategic / Temporistics | Where are we going, and how do memory, hope, fear, direction, and meaning shape us? | temporal frame, not values or destiny |
| Operational / Psychosophy | How do we choose, reason, express feelings, carry responsibility, and care for practical life? | action organization, not maturity rank |
| Tactical / Socionics | How do we speak, listen, exchange information, misunderstand, and repair? | information modeling, not all communication |

Context applies across every level. Consent and safety constrain every interpretation; they are not extra typological levels.

<!-- section:typology-system-decisions -->
## Typology-System Decisions

<!-- section:socionics -->
### Socionics

**Decision:** research route first; translate into human questions in ordinary Christian copy.

Use questions about how people exchange information, emphasize different details, misunderstand wording, and repair conversation. Keep Model A, information-element codes, quadras, function positions, and relation names in deeper research pages. Mark Socionics as a heuristic source tradition, not validated Christian anthropology or spiritual insight.

<!-- section:psychosophy -->
### Psychosophy

**Decision:** research route first; translate domains into action questions.

- Will → how responsibility, initiative, authority, and decision pressure are carried;
- Logic → how reasoning and explanations are developed;
- Emotion → how feelings and emotional expression are handled;
- Physics → how body, home, money, resources, and practical needs are cared for.

Do not equate Will with obedience, Logic with wisdom, Emotion with love, Physics with materialism, or position with maturity.

<!-- section:temporistics -->
### Temporistics

**Decision:** research route first; translate aspects into temporal and meaning questions.

- Past → memory, story, wounds, gratitude, forgiveness, and learning;
- Present → place, belonging, attention, current obedience, and contact;
- Future → direction, planning, hope, fear, and promises;
- Eternity → meaning and ultimate questions before God.

Explain that “Eternity” is a research-category name, not a doctrinal conclusion. Do not present temporal positions or archetypes as calling, destiny, or spiritual condition.

<!-- section:application-decisions -->
## Application Decisions

<!-- section:serious-relationships-and-marriage -->
### Serious Relationships and Marriage

Lead with shared faith, character, maturity, consent, safety, honest expectations, prayer, wise counsel, and responsibility. Translate compatibility into “questions before a serious step” and any result into “themes to discuss.”

Avoid claims about a chosen spouse, guaranteed family outcome, ideal pair, or a type-based decision. Claims about marriage, divorce, unequal yoking, church authority, or God's will for a specific relationship require **PASTOR CHECK**.

<!-- section:family -->
### Family

Lead with patience, truthfulness, forgiveness, repair, trust, household responsibility, and safety. Translate “interaction pattern” into “where we may repeatedly help, pressure, or misunderstand each other.” Never diagnose relatives by type, and never use forgiveness language to minimize coercion or abuse.

<!-- section:church-service -->
### Church Service

Lead with one body and many members, gifts, limits, humility, mutual need, faithful service, and care. Translate “role fit” into “where responsibility may be carried faithfully.” Any wording that assigns spiritual status, ministry worth, or authority from a type requires rejection or **PASTOR CHECK**.

<!-- section:team-and-common-work -->
### Team and Common Work

Lead with clear agreements, workload, trust, decision rights, responsibility, and conflict repair. People are not resources or optimization units. A role hypothesis must be tested through skill, consent, observation, support needs, and context.

<!-- section:business-partnership -->
### Business Partnership

Lead with honesty, money, power, risk, promises, decision rights, accountability, and exit rules. Typology cannot settle questions of integrity, debt, authority, legal responsibility, or whether to enter a partnership.

<!-- section:ai-boundary -->
## AI Boundary

In Christian-facing Before We Build, AI may only support low-authority tasks:

- summarize material the user has chosen to provide;
- organize the user's own notes and questions;
- suggest alternative questions or explanations for human review;
- keep sources, caveats, and uncertainty visible.

AI must never imitate another person, replace their participation or consent, predict a person or relationship, claim spiritual discernment, or issue a compatibility decision. It cannot determine character, safety, truthfulness, God's will, or future conduct from typological material.

<!-- section:page-routing-decisions -->
## Page-Routing Decisions

- Public Christian entry pages should begin with foundation, character, consent, safety, conversation, prayer, wisdom, and counsel.
- Core orientation and theory pages must remain universal and clearly link to the Christian application as a specialization.
- Technical typology entity and relation pages remain available through an intentional research route, not the default Christian route.
- A public link to a type or relation page must introduce it as a possible theme for conversation, not “who you are” or “your outcome.”
- The measurement roadmap may explain research prerequisites but must not offer a current formula, weight, percentage, or pair verdict.

<!-- section:required-editorial-checks -->
## Required Editorial Checks

For every Christian-facing edit, verify:

1. Is the value-moral foundation clearly separate from the strategic Temporistics level?
2. Are typology claims marked as heuristic hypotheses rather than facts about a person?
3. Are observation, interpretation, alternative explanations, and practical guidance distinguishable?
4. Are consent, power, coercion, abuse, violence, and safety visible where relevant?
5. Does any wording imply a numerical or automatic compatibility judgment?
6. Does AI stay within summarization, question organization, and human-reviewed alternatives?
7. Are Christian normative claims grounded nearby in Scripture and sent for pastoral review when needed?
8. Is the universal core still understandable to a reader who does not share the Christian application?

<!-- section:open-pastor-human-review-questions -->
## Open Pastor / Human Review Questions

1. Which page should be the central exposition of 1 Corinthians 12:13–22 for the application?
2. How should mature counsel be described so it supports church care without enabling control?
3. What wording best distinguishes shared faith from a promise of relationship success?
4. When should “Eternity” appear as a disclosed research term rather than the public word “meaning”?
5. Which claims about marriage, family, service, authority, or repair require a standing pastoral review rule?

<!-- section:final-recommendation -->
## Final Recommendation

The default translation for Christian-facing content is:

```text
research term → concrete human question → Scripture-near framing
→ honest conversation → human judgment with consent, safety, prayer, and counsel
```

Do not expose a technical label unless the reader intentionally opens the research layer. The application should help people ask truer questions and notice relevant evidence; it must not claim to see the heart, predict another person, rank dignity, or deliver a relationship verdict.
