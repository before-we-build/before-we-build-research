# Start Here

English · [[start-here-ru|Русский]] · [[start-here-uk|Українська]]

<!-- section:in-90-seconds -->
## In 90 seconds

Before We Build is a research framework for asking how two people may build, decide, coordinate, and sustain a relationship or shared task. It separates a value-moral foundation from three proposed typological lenses: strategic direction through Temporistics, operational joint action through Psychosophy, and tactical information exchange through Socionics. These lenses organize questions; they do not diagnose a person or deliver a compatibility verdict.

Here a type means a model hypothesis about a pattern of perception and experience organization, not a personality type. “Perception” includes selecting, ordering, interpreting, and organizing experience, not only sensory perception. See [[typology-reconceptualization-en]].

<!-- section:definition-and-scope -->
## Definition and scope

The framework can be used for partners, friends, relatives, colleagues, founders, or other pairs when the domain and desired outcome are named. Context runs across all four levels. Consent, coercion, violence, exploitation, and safety are gates, not style differences.

<!-- section:shared-example -->
## Shared example

Two people are considering a move to another city. Before We Build separates their non-negotiable obligations, their sense of future direction, their way of dividing decisions and effort, and their concrete exchange of information. It also asks what finances, health, dependants, power, and safety change about the decision.

<!-- section:observations -->
## What can be observed

Their statements, choices, plans, task division, questions, misunderstandings, conduct under pressure, and attempts at repair can be observed. A type or latent process cannot be observed directly.

<!-- section:hypotheses -->
## What remains a hypothesis

A typological pattern may suggest a process worth investigating. It remains revisable and must compete with explanations based on experience, culture, skills, role, stress, health, and circumstance.

<!-- section:alternatives -->
## Alternative explanations

The same disagreement may reflect different values, limited resources, unequal power, missing information, a temporary crisis, or a learned habit. A type label is never the default explanation.

<!-- section:non-inferences -->
## What must not be inferred

No type establishes moral character, dignity, safety, destiny, readiness for marriage, or the success of a team. Before We Build currently provides no numerical compatibility score.

<!-- section:conversation-questions -->
## Questions for conversation

- What exactly are we trying to build?
- Which commitments cannot be traded away?
- What evidence supports our interpretation of the disagreement?
- What would change our minds?
- Is anyone unable to consent or speak freely?
