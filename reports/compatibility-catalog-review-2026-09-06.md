Wiki summaries are `derived-synthesis`; explicitly separated BWB implications are project research proposals, not source findings or typology validation. No new raw full-text archive was audited.

## Independent primary-record spot checks

1. **Roddy et al. 2020** — [authors' abstract indexed in PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32551734/). Confirmed 58 studies, 40 distinct samples, 2,092 couples, and within-treatment pre/post Hedges' g = 1.12. The card correctly distinguishes this from a controlled active-treatment comparison and preserves opposite-sex-sample and dependence limitations. Evidence: primary-source abstract for the meta-analysis, not independently re-extracted participant evidence.
2. **Hawkins et al. 2022** — [authors' abstract indexed in PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35040124/). Confirmed 32 control-group studies, 19 additional single-group studies, relationship-quality d = .114 and skills d = .132, and nonsignificant stability, parenting and child-well-being effects. The card preserves the controlled versus pre/post distinction and does not equate nonsignificance with proof of zero benefit. Evidence: primary-source abstract.
3. **Kim et al. 2007** — [authors' abstract indexed in PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17372624/). Confirmed 85 couples, approximately 2.5-year follow-up, unsuccessful generalization of major Gottman et al. **1998** findings, and topic-related interaction differences. The card expressly avoids presenting this as a direct replication of the adjacent 2000 paper. Evidence: primary-source abstract.
4. **Dyrenforth et al. 2010, attempted check** — [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20718544/) produced no usable rendered abstract; the [DIW institution route](https://www.diw.de/de/diw_01.c.767528.de/s_6043.html) timed out. Secondary search results were not substituted for primary verification. The author's access log discloses a similar restriction and reports its own indexed-abstract/institution cross-check. Evidence status for this review: author-documented provenance, independently unverified numerical extraction.

## Counts, translations, and inference boundaries

- Exactly 8 study sections were found per source collection, yielding **24 distinct DOI identifiers**, with no duplicated publication DOI across the three English collections. This establishes unique publications, not independent underlying datasets.
- Within each source triad, checked semantic versions, reviewed versions, roles, claims, caveat IDs, source lists, ordered section IDs and DOI sets matched. Translated findings inspected retained outcome direction and numerical results. This structural and sampled semantic check does not claim exhaustive translation certification.
- Source frontmatter points to the studies summarized in the cards. Alternative full-text/access routes can appear in author search records. After the inspected hub version, the owner reports adding a raw bibliographic snapshot of the 24 works and linking it from hub frontmatter. The new snapshot was not independently audited in this pass; research claims remain routed to source pages.
- The catalog identifies its selection as non-exhaustive and explicitly disclaims systematic screening and typology validation. Cards separate attraction, satisfaction, continuation, friendship and team performance. Team-to-pair transfer requires further testing.
- The summaries preserve observational versus intervention distinctions, within-group versus controlled effects, replication failures, population limits and historical dates. BWB applications are described as proposals; safety, consent and moral worth are not inferred from measured relationship outcomes.
