## Proposed explanation

Working hypothesis: some people provisionally coded 1F/4F may prefer a clear direction they can formulate or join; some provisionally coded 2F/3F may prefer opportunities to clarify or check an individual's changing path. Their motivations need not match within either pair. In particular, 4F does not imply optimism, while 3F may involve private checking, reassurance-seeking or avoidance rather than enjoyment of uncertainty or external dialogue.

The bridge to age is conditional: the reported association might arise if the observed churches connect youth ministry with a shared project and older-adult ministry with individual accompaniment. Those formats have not been observed or measured in the supplied report. Either age group can take part in either format; real ministries may combine them.

Illustrative counter-scenarios, not collected cases: 2F youth mentoring, 4F routine older-adult support, 1F building a project for older adults, and 3F avoiding contact that heightens fear. These possibilities prevent the proposed explanation from becoming an automatic inference from age or type.

## What could distinguish the explanation

Measure format separately from beneficiary age, before inspecting position associations. Compare comparable formats across age groups and different formats within age groups. Examine each of the four positions separately. A precise lack of the predicted format differences, reversed differences, or a persistent age pattern without the proposed format link would weaken this explanation. Smaller age differences after accounting for format would be compatible with it, but would not by itself establish causal mediation. Original age hypothesis H1 and proposed format hypothesis H2 can fail independently.
