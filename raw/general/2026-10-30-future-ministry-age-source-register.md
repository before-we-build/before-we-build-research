# Future position and age of church-ministry recipients: source register

Captured: 2026-10-30. This dated intake preserves a user-origin observation, source pointers and original summaries. It contains no participant records or copied articles. Add later evidence in a new dated record rather than rewriting the original report.

## Origin and scope

In the repository conversation, the user reported an apparent pattern: people interpreted as having first or fourth Future in Temporistics serve young people, while those interpreted as having second or third Future serve older adults. The user subsequently clarified that the context is **church ministry**. This paraphrase records what the user noticed; the repository has not independently observed or quantified it.

No sample size, group denominators, ages, church information, position-assessment method, chronology, counterexamples or available ministry opportunities were supplied. It is unknown whether participation reflected preference, invitation, appointment, family duties or availability. No association magnitude or population frequency can be calculated. The report does not establish calling, competence, spiritual maturity or a basis for assigning ministry.

## Search boundary and decisions

Search directions included `"Temporistics" volunteering`, `"темпористика" "первое будущее" молодеж`, `темпористика будущее молодежь пожилые служение исследование`, `future time perspective volunteering older adults younger people generativity beneficiary age study`, and `future time perspective preference volunteering older adults beneficiaries study`. This is a selected literature search, not a systematic review. No direct test of the reported church-ministry association was located in the inspected results; this does not prove that no such study exists.

Do not equate future orientation, perceived time remaining, generativity, volunteer age, recipient age or Temporistics position. In particular, the grouping 1F/4F does not mean a longer horizon, and 2F/3F does not mean a shorter one. None of the five papers measures these four positions or validates the complete proposed grouping.

Accepted: preservation of the observation, attribution of the 3F source motif, and adjacent study-specific findings. Open: existence, direction, size, mechanism and generalizability of the association. Rejected: typing from ministry, assigning roles by type, spiritual ranking and an assumed equivalence between age and temporal orientation.
