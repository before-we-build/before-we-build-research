## Search boundary and decisions

Selected exploratory search, not a systematic review: English/Russian queries combined migraine/мигрень with forecasting ability, predictive processing, interoception, episodic future thinking and Temporistics/темпористика. Repository searches included мигрен/мігрен/migraine and прогнозатор. Search hits concerned neural/body prediction, attack forecasting, or algorithmic prediction; those constructs cannot be pooled as evidence of external-event forecasting skill. No direct first-Future–migraine study or superior external forecasting test was located in this search. This is a search outcome, not proof of universal absence.

Accepted: adjacent, study-specific findings and clear construct distinctions. Open: the user-origin association and possible explanations. Unsupported: treating migraine as a 1F indicator, establishing biological typing, claiming prognostic giftedness, or assigning a migraine cause from a profile. No clinical diagnosis, participant recruitment, data collection or treatment recommendation occurred.
