# Compatibility research catalog — source ledger

Research date: 2026-09-06

## Scope and provenance

First-release bibliographic snapshot: 24 selected peer-reviewed publications, not a systematic review or exhaustive inventory. Entries below retain publication metadata and source links used for the derived EN/RU/UK cards. This is an original research record, not a copy of the papers. Full-text and abstract access differ; search records document the material actually inspected. DOI links identify publications but do not imply full-text access. No pooled analysis, formal risk-of-bias assessment, or verification of typological predictions was performed.

Later corrections should be recorded in a dated successor record; preserve this snapshot.

## Accepted uses and limits

The sources provide context-specific findings and methodological baselines for attraction, satisfaction, relationship continuation, friendship, and teamwork. Reviews may include overlapping primary samples: neither participants nor effect sizes should be summed across these cards. BWB implications in the wiki are project interpretations. These sources do not validate BWB typologies, identify an ideal partner, establish moral worth, or equate relationship continuation with consent and safety. Commercial promises and inaccessible detailed results are not treated as verified findings.
