# Aušra Augustinavičiūtė — Information-Metabolism Foundations: Provenance Record

## Purpose

This record documents the source set used to audit the Before We Build
reconstruction of Socionics information aspects. It does not reproduce the
complete copyrighted works and does not treat a school publication as
independent empirical validation.

## Audit findings

- The author texts treat information elements as differentiated aspects of an
  objective world and describe type differences through selection,
  differentiation, compression, processing, and exchange of information.
- The source vocabulary is partly energetic and spatial: potential and kinetic
  energy, activity, excitation, time, space, objective ratios, and attraction
  or repulsion.
- The source-oriented three-axis scaffold used for this audit is:
  object/field, static/dynamic, and internal/external.
- On that scaffold the working attribution is: Ne internal-object-static; Se
  external-object-static; Fe internal-object-dynamic; Te
  external-object-dynamic; Ni internal-field-dynamic; Si
  external-field-dynamic; Fi internal-field-static; Ti
  external-field-static.
- `raw/socionics/information-aspects.md` assigns static/dynamic differently for
  Si, Ne, Fi, and Fe. That archived file remains unchanged. Derived pages must
  disclose the discrepancy and must not silently use it as the canonical
  source-axis map.

## Interpretation boundary

The Before We Build language of partial reality models, selection,
compression, inference, error signals, and updating is a project
reconstruction. It is not a quotation from Augustinavičiūtė, proof that the
eight constructs exist, or evidence that they are exhaustive, discrete,
innate, independent, or neurally localized.
