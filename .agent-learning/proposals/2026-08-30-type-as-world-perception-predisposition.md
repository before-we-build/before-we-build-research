# Agent Improvement Proposal: Type as a Process Predisposition Hypothesis

## Observed opportunity

Agents already reject deterministic personality verdicts, but their shared
positive definition of a type is incomplete. This can leave users with the
traditional impression that Socionics, Psychosophy, or Temporistics classify
whole personalities.

## Approved instruction change

- Describe a type in Before We Build as a compact model hypothesis about a
  pattern of world perception, experience structuring, or process
  organization, never as a personality type or kind of person.
- Use the plain-language label "type of perception and experience
  organization" and retain the technical labels "type pattern" and
  "latent-process hypothesis."
- Treat a natural predisposition as a separate research hypothesis; never
  present it as demonstrated biological innateness.
- Preserve the system-specific mappings: Socionics to information modeling,
  Psychosophy to analysis/synthesis/action organization, and Temporistics to
  abductive, inductive, and deductive structuring of temporal/existential
  experience.
- Compare natural-origin interpretations with learning, role, culture, state,
  stress, and context.

## Risk assessment

Risk is `moderate`: the wording could accidentally introduce an unsupported
biological claim. The required caveat reduces that risk and does not weaken
existing safety, dignity, diagnostic, or compatibility limits.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The definition separates a type pattern from the whole personality.
- [x] Natural predisposition is labeled as an unvalidated hypothesis.
- [x] The three typologies retain distinct proposed processes.
- [x] Agents continue to require observations, alternatives, and
  falsification conditions.

## Rollback

Revert the shared positive-definition paragraph while retaining the existing
non-determinism rules. Do not remove the caveat about unestablished
innateness.
