# Review decision

The revised proposal is approved by both the source/provenance and
system-runtime passes.

## Source and provenance

- The preserved raw architecture, platform-independent protocol, and immutable
  pre-adaptation skill revision are identified separately.
- Intentional treatment of the uncalibrated `lambda(s) > 0.40` gate, the fixed
  `4±1` concept count, and the two adjacent protocols is explicit.
- The five-level BWB inference distinction is retained.
- No scientific assertion was added; numerical certainty was reduced.

## Runtime behavior

- Unavailable `invoke_subagent` and nested CLI calls are replaced by the
  available collaboration mechanism.
- Audit remains read-only, while Revision is limited to an explicitly authorized
  target.
- Five composite roles can run in parallel waves or combined passes with
  distinguishable judgments.
- Scorecards and reader-retention estimates are explicitly labeled as simulated
  model judgments.
- Structural validation and the realistic behavioral dry run passed with no
  blocking defect.

Activation may proceed under the project owner's explicit installation request,
followed by the repository CI suite.
