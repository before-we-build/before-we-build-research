# Typology-based role assignment removal review

## Decision basis

The owner-approved migration makes types non-deterministic model hypotheses and expressly forbids inference from type to profession, safety, dignity, destiny, or guaranteed outcome. It also requires auditing `.opencode/agents/` for obsolete and ambiguous guidance.

## Review decision

- [x] Remove direct type-to-career and type-to-military mappings.
- [x] Preserve useful role-taxonomy and current-role research only as external context.
- [x] Require direct evidence and informed human decision-making.
- [x] Treat military and other high-risk placement as outside exploratory typology.
