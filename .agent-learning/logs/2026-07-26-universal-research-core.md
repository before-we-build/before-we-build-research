# Universal Research Core Reframing Log

## Trigger

The project owner clarified that Before We Build, as a research project,
should model compatibility between any two people rather than define its whole
ontology through one Christian audience or application.

The owner approved **value-moral level** (`ценностно-нравственный уровень`) as
the universal name for the foundational compatibility level.

## Observed architecture problem

Core orientation and governance pages currently collapse two scopes:

1. a universal compatibility research architecture;
2. a Christian relationship and family-discernment application.

This causes universal theory pages to require Scripture, church context, and
Christian identity even when the research subject is an abstract pair of
people anywhere in the world.

## Decision

Adopt a two-layer project boundary:

```text
Before We Build universal research core
→ worldview/domain applications
→ Christian relationship discernment as the first developed application
```

The universal foundational level becomes **value-moral / foundational**. It
includes worldview, ultimate commitments, moral obligations, consent,
dignity, truth, responsibility, reciprocity, repair, and non-negotiable
boundaries.

Christian pages remain valid as a specialization that interprets this level
through Christ, Scripture, observable fruit, church community, and mature
counsel.

## Safety constraints

- Universal does not mean value-free or that all values are equivalent.
- Consent, coercion, abuse, violence, exploitation, and human dignity remain
  explicit safety constraints.
- The value-moral level is not a typology or automated moral score.
- The Christian specialization must not be erased or presented as a claim
  about all possible users.
- Typological mappings remain heuristic and non-deterministic.
