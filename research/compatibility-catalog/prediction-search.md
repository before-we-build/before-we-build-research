## Search question and selection

What findings distinguish initial attraction, existing relationship quality, future dissolution and change following interventions? Eight selected entry points for the first 24-paper catalog; this is not an exhaustive or preregistered systematic review.

Include peer-reviewed journal work with verified primary journal, PubMed/PMC or author records; positive and null results; exactly three intervention meta-analyses. Exclude media summaries as evidence, commercial testimonials, unverified numbers and typology-community claims presented as empirical validation. No individual treatment advice is provided. Full bibliography, journal, year and DOI are in each synchronized card.

## Baselines and limitations

Suggested research variables: initial desire, actor/partner tendencies, satisfaction level and change, commitment, observed communication, relationship duration, dissolution horizon and intervention exposure. Forecasting later level differs from forecasting change. General popularity is not a pair-specific effect. Pre/post change differs from randomized comparison.

Full text was checked for Joel 2020 and Finkel 2012; Gottman 2000 has partial article access; five cards rely on abstracts. This search performed no exhaustive database export, risk-of-bias appraisal or deduplication of review samples. Card author checked cross-language semantic equivalence; this is not an independent provenance audit.

Provenance audit priorities: full methods for abstract-only cards; external validation and model-selection details before reusing the Gottman accuracy estimate; overlap among intervention reviews; population representation and follow-up attrition. Do not sum review samples as independent participants or count overlapping reviews as independent replications. The 2022 Hawkins review is not a direct replication of the 2008 review. Differences between their estimates cannot be assigned to a single cause without further analysis.

No paper tests BWB typologies or the four-level architecture. These sources support research design questions, not claims that BWB tools work. Safety and consent cannot be inferred from stability or satisfaction alone.
