# Couples literature search — 2026-09-06

## Search question

Which peer-reviewed syntheses and influential primary studies help distinguish romantic attraction, satisfaction, similarity, attachment, and relationship continuation, and supply baseline comparisons for BWB?

## Method and scope

Targeted historical seed search, not a systematic review. Web search privileged journal publishers, PubMed, PMC and author institutions. Included identifiable peer-reviewed papers with accessible abstracts or text, clear question/design, and direct relevance to the named outcomes. Excluded blogs, unsourced popular claims, typology-community assertions, and papers assigned to other catalog owners (Joel 2017/2020, Finkel 2012, Gottman/Levenson 2000, Kim 2007). No claim of exhaustive or current-to-2026 coverage. Search used English terms and therefore may miss non-English research. No pooled estimate or formal risk-of-bias score calculated.

## Evidence handoff and further provenance review

The wiki separates attributed findings from BWB proposals. Before using effect sizes in a quantitative analysis, provenance review should inspect full methods, distinguish people from couples, audit overlap among meta-analyses, and check effect-size extraction and uncertainty. Particularly unresolved: Luo profile similarity versus Dyrenforth incremental similarity; effects of self-report versus observation in Li; observational temporal direction versus intervention efficacy in Lavner. The seed set covers attitudes through Luo but is not a comprehensive review of moral values, religious homogamy, or all relationship populations.

## Suggested baseline variables

Both partners' continuous personality traits, attachment anxiety/avoidance, independently measured and perceived similarity, satisfaction, relationship duration/stage, commitment, investments, alternatives, observed interaction, social-network support, external circumstances, and separate continuation outcomes. These are proposed research comparisons, not a validated BWB instrument or partner verdict.

## Triad review

EN/RU/UK cards were drafted and compared for matching bibliographic identifiers, sample statements, outcome distinctions, caveats, and BWB inference boundaries. This is author semantic review, not an independent specialist audit.
